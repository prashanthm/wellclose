# Data licenses & terms (Risk §14.4)
- **BSEE/BOEM, Texas RRC**: US public records. Polite automated access per §4.5 (rate limits,
  honest UA, robots honored — note webapps.rrc.texas.gov disallows crawling and is not
  accessed; the Neubus imaged-records service and the published orphan-inventory download
  are used instead). No login-walled or paywalled content.

  resale; verified at https://data.equinor.com/dataset/Volve 2026-06-10) — NO redistribution
  of raw data in demos/marketing without re-checking terms (Risk §14.4); we ship only derived
  facts with citations pointing at the licensee's local copy.
